Our commitment to your privacy

Quran Glow does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use the application.

We do not directly collect or maintain a database of users' personal information.

📱

2. Device Permissions

Quran Glow does not request sensitive device permissions such as access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

Any permissions required for basic application functionality will be limited to what is necessary.

Quran Glow currently does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking services.
